I used to think the moon followed me.

When I was younger, I would walk home and watch it move between the trees. No matter where I went, it remained above me.

I thought it was watching.

I know now that I was wrong.

It wasn't following me.

I was following it.

On the night of the thirteenth phase, I looked up and saw something where the moon should have been.

I have spent the rest of my life trying to forget it.

LUNAR VEIL told me forgetting was impossible.

They lied.

— C.
